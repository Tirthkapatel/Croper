Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Flipkart/Amazon Label Cropper. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and business information.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: We use Google Authentication (Firebase) to create and manage your account. We store your email address and basic profile information provided by Google.
  • Inventory Data: We store the product SKUs and quantities that you process through the app in our secure database (Firestore) to provide you with inventory tracking features.
  • PDF Files: The PDF files you upload are processed locally in your browser. We do NOT upload, store, or transmit your PDF files or shipping labels to our servers. Your customer data remains completely private.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the features of this application (such as tracking your processed inventory and allowing you to transfer data to other users). We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

4. Data Transfer

If you choose to use the "Data Transfer" feature, you consent to transferring a copy of your inventory data to the specified recipient email address.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures provided by Google Firebase to protect your account and database information.
