Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

Welcome to the Flipkart/Amazon Label Cropper & Inventory toolkit. To provide our services, we collect:

  • Account Data: We use Google Authentication (via Firebase) for secure login. We store your email address, display name, and basic profile info.
  • Inventory Data: Product SKUs, initial stock, and dispatched quantities that you track are stored securely in our database (Firestore) to persist your dashboard data.
  • Uploaded PDFs: The shipping labels and PDF files you upload for cropping are processed entirely locally in your browser. We do NOT upload, store, or transmit your actual PDF files or customer shipping data to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide and improve this application's features, such as tracking your daily seller performance and securely synchronizing your inventory across sessions. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

We leverage industry-standard security measures provided by Google Firebase to encrypt and protect your account and database information. Your data is isolated and cannot be accessed by other users.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (provided by Google) as our backend infrastructure. These services comply with strict international privacy and security standards.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete all your SKUs and inventory history directly from the Settings dashboard using the "Clear All User Data" option. To permanently delete your entire account, use the "Delete Account" button in the Danger Zone.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@labelcropper.test.
